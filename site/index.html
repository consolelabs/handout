Your Mac is the server

Handout runs a small web server on your Mac and opens an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel to it. Visitors are served straight from your machine.

Questions, answered

The short version of how Handout behaves. The README has the rest.

Is it free?

Yes. Handout is open source under the MIT licence. The free address uses Cloudflare's free tunnels, and a domain of your own on Cloudflare costs nothing extra for this.

Do my files leave my Mac?

Only while someone is opening them. Each request travels through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel to your Mac, which sends back that one file. Nothing is stored on a Handout server, because there isn't one.

What happens when my Mac sleeps?

Links pause while your Mac sleeps and answer again when it wakes. A free address can change after a long sleep. Handout notices, gets a new one and tells you, so you can copy your links again.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, any domain on Cloudflare. Links on your domain keep their address for good, can be limited to people who sign in, and can be shared with a team.

Is there a command line version?

Handout started as one. The app uses the same handout command underneath, so a link made in the menu bar shows up in the terminal and the other way round.
